# Customer Operations Guide

This guide helps customers navigate the Parichay portal after logging in.

## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account
1. Visit the homepage and click "Start Free"
2. Enter your first name, last name, email, and password
3. Accept the Terms of Service
4. Your 14-day free trial starts immediately

### Logging In
1. Go to `/login`
2. Enter your email and password
3. You'll be redirected to your dashboard

## Your Dashboard (`/customer-dashboard`)

After login, you'll see:
- **Favorite Businesses** — Businesses you've saved
- **Services Used** — History of services you've booked
- **Pending Services** — Upcoming appointments
- **Average Rating** — Your average review score

## Finding Businesses

### Search (`/search`)
- Search by business name, service type, or location
- Filter by industry, rating, distance
- View results on map or list view

### Business Profiles
Each business profile shows:
- Business name, description, and rating
- Services with pricing
- Photo gallery
- Location with Google Maps directions
- Contact buttons (Call, WhatsApp, Email)
- Business hours
- Customer reviews

## Interacting with Businesses

### Contact a Business
- **Call**: Tap the phone button for direct call
- **WhatsApp**: Opens WhatsApp with pre-filled message
- **Email**: Opens your email client
- **Directions**: Opens Google Maps from your current location

### Book an Appointment
1. Find the business profile
2. Look for "Book Appointment" or "Book a Table" button
3. Select date, time, and service
4. Confirm booking
5. You'll receive confirmation via SMS/WhatsApp

### Submit a Review
1. Visit the business profile
2. Scroll to "Customer Reviews" section
3. Select star rating (1-5)
4. Write your feedback
5. Complete the security check (math captcha)
6. Click "Submit Review"
7. Your review will appear after moderation

### Save to Favorites
- Click the heart icon on any business profile
- Access saved businesses from your dashboard

## Service History

### Viewing Past Services
- Navigate to "Service History" tab on your dashboard
- See all services you've used with dates and status
- Status types: Completed, Pending, Cancelled

### Rating Past Services
- From service history, click on a completed service
- Leave a rating and optional feedback
- Helps businesses improve their service

## QR Code Scanning

When you see a Parichay QR code at a business:
1. Open your phone camera
2. Point at the QR code
3. Tap the link that appears
4. You'll see the business's full digital profile
5. Save contact, book appointment, or leave a review

## Notifications

### Types of Notifications
- Appointment confirmations
- Service reminders
- Special offers from favorite businesses
- Review responses from businesses

### Managing Notifications
1. Go to "Preferences" tab in your dashboard
2. Toggle notification types:
   - Email notifications for appointment reminders
   - SMS notifications for service updates
   - Marketing emails about new services

## Privacy & Settings

### Location Settings
- Enable/disable location for nearby search
- Control whether distance shows in results

### Privacy Controls
- Control if businesses can see your contact info
- Toggle service history sharing for recommendations

### Account Deletion
1. Go to Settings
2. Click "Delete Account"
3. Confirm with your email
4. Your account will be soft-deleted with 30-day recovery window
5. After 30 days, data is permanently removed

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is my data safe?**
A: Yes. Your phone number is encrypted at rest in our database. All communication is over HTTPS. We follow GDPR-compliant data handling practices.

**Q: How do I change my password?**
A: Go to `/forgot-password`, enter your email, and follow the reset link sent to your inbox.

**Q: Can businesses see my personal information?**
A: Only if you explicitly allow it in Privacy Settings. By default, only your name is shared when you submit a review or booking.

**Q: How do I report a business?**
A: Contact us at support@parichay.io with the business name and your concern.

**Q: Is there a mobile app?**
A: Parichay works as a Progressive Web App (PWA). Add it to your home screen from your browser for an app-like experience.
